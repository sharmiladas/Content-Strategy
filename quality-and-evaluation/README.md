# Quality and evaluation

## A review packet for higher-risk AI content

A reviewer should not have to reconstruct the system before making a decision. For higher-risk material, provide:

- The original request and generated answer
- The sources used, including dates, versions, and owners
- Results for factual accuracy, source use, audience fit, policy compliance, and task completion
- The reason for any failed check
- The risk tier and the decision the reviewer is being asked to make
- A route for sending repeated problems back to the source owner

This packet does not automate judgment. It reduces avoidable detective work and leaves a clearer record of why an output was approved, changed, or rejected.

Related essay: [AI Quality Starts Before the Final Review](https://sharmiladas.github.io/articles/quality.html).
