# Content-Audit Template

Begin with one customer task, product area, or journey. A bounded audit is easier to finish and more likely to produce a decision.

| Field | What to record |
|---|---|
| Customer question or task | The need this content should address |
| Source | The authoritative information behind the content |
| Asset and location | Title, URL, repository path, or system |
| Audience and market | Who it serves and where it applies |
| Product and version | The scope and release boundary |
| Owner | Who can approve and maintain the underlying information |
| Lifecycle state | Planned, current, stale, duplicate, or retire |
| Last reviewed | Date and reviewer |
| Performance signal | Search, use, completion, support impact, feedback, or none available |
| Reuse opportunity | Where the same approved information could support another experience |
| Risk or gap | Contradiction, missing coverage, weak evidence, unclear ownership, or another issue |
| Next decision | Keep, update, combine, create, or retire |

## After the inventory

Group findings by decision rather than producing a long spreadsheet summary. A useful readout answers:

- Which contradictions create customer or business risk?
- Which repeated work is worth consolidating?
- Which gaps block an important task?
- Which ownership questions require a sponsor?
- What will be measured after the change?
